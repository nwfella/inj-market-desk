Method — so you can argue with it

  • Source. Order books and market metadata: Injective public LCD, sentry.lcd.injective.network, keyless, no rate-limit account. 24h volume: CoinGecko's injective_futures tickers (third-party, refreshed every 30 min).
  • Price normalisation. Derivative book prices are chain-scaled by 1e6; verified against spot at snapshot time (BTC mid 81,291 vs spot 81,262).
  • Depth. Notional resting within X bps of the touch (best bid / best ask), taking the thinner side — the side a taker can actually use. Measuring from mid would score a 60 bps-wide market as zero-depth, which is misleading.
  • Spread. (ask − bid) / mid × 10,000, live touch only. Round trip = spread + 2 × taker fee from market metadata.
  • Health score (0–100), weighted: two-sided quote 25 · spread 25 · depth ≤25 bps 25 · 24h volume 15 · book balance 10. Sub-scores are linear/log maps with published breakpoints: spread 2 bps→1.0, 200 bps→0; depth $1k→0, $1M→1.0; volume $10k→0, $1M→1.0. Weights renormalise when an input is missing.
  • Known gaps. No funding rate, open interest, maker identity, or historical series yet — those need the indexer's gRPC-web surface, which is Phase 1. One snapshot; spreads move.
Built from public chain state. Not investment advice; grades describe book microstructure at one moment, not the creditworthiness of any issuer. source
